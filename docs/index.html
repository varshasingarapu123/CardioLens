Applied Medical Technology · Research Prototype

From raw ECG signals to interpretable evidence.

CardioLens is an explainable ECG intelligence research prototype designed to turn public ECG signals into reproducible machine-learning experiments and signal-level attribution evidence.

Research design

CardioLens emphasizes reproducibility and honest reporting: no hard-coded accuracy claims, no synthetic clinical outcomes, and no real-patient deployment.

Medical safety & limitations

This is a research and educational prototype, not a medical device and not intended for diagnosis or clinical decision-making. MIT-BIH is a historical public dataset and does not represent every patient, device, or clinical environment. Dataset performance must not be interpreted as clinical performance.
